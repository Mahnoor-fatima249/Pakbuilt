Typically, a standard 5-marla grey structure takes around 3 to 4 months to complete, depending on labor availability and weather conditions.
Grey structure includes earthwork, foundation, brick masonry, R.C.C work (roofs, beams, columns), plaster, underground water tanks, and basic plumbing/electric piping.
