Construction Tips

Grey Structure

Foundation depth should be minimum 5 feet for single storey, 7 feet for double storey in Lahore soil conditions.

Material Tip

Always buy cement from authorized dealers. Check manufacturing date - cement loses 20% strength after 3 months.

Budget Tip

Keep 10-15% buffer of total budget for unexpected expenses during construction.
